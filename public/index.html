Important Guest Notes

Before the curtain rises.

A few important notes to help every guest arrive ready for an unforgettable celebration.

What is required for entry?

Physical or digital RSVP verification is required for entry. Please have your confirmation email or screen available when you arrive.

Can I bring a guest?

Plus-ones are subject to host approval because capacity is limited. Choose “Me + 1” in the RSVP form and add your guest’s full name. You’ll get an email once the host confirms.

How do I change or cancel my RSVP?

Use the “View or change your RSVP” link in your confirmation email. Lost it? Open the RSVP page, enter your email under “Already RSVP’d?”, and we’ll send it again. Changes are open until March 18, 2027.

Are cards, gifts, or contributions welcome?

Cards, gifts, and contributions honoring Tootie Jay’s milestone celebration are welcome.

What should guests know about beverages and refreshments?

Beverage and cocktail service will follow event service protocols. Please enjoy responsibly, and plan a safe ride home. Rideshare buttons are in the Venue section.

Is the venue accessible?

Let us know any accessibility needs in your RSVP, such as accessible parking, a drop-off at the door, or seating, and the host will confirm the arrangements with Kitty Hawk Event Center.

Who should I contact with an urgent question?

Call or text host Tenisia Ruiz at (347) 401-5569, or reply to your RSVP confirmation email.

Privacy notice

This site is run by the host, Tenisia Ruiz, for one private celebration.

  • What we collect: what you type into the RSVP and memory-wall forms. That’s your name, email, phone, attendance, plus-one name, meal, song, dietary, and accessibility notes.
  • Why: to verify the guest list at the door, plan food and seating, and email you your confirmation and event updates.
  • Where it’s kept: RSVPs are collected on the Double Nickel guest page and kept privately by the host. Only the host can see them. Your contact details are never shown publicly.
  • Party updates: if you sign up for updates, we keep your name, email and optional phone to send news about this event only. Ask to be removed anytime.
  • Memory wall: only your name and message are shown publicly, and only after the host approves them.
  • How long: the host deletes guest contact details within 60 days after the event.
  • Your choice: reply to your confirmation email to have your details corrected or removed at any time.
